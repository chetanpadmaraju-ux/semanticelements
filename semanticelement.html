Why your body needs exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes every day are happier, sleep better, and do better at school.

Dancing, swimming, football, yoga — anything that gets your heart beating faster counts. Keep moving and have fun!

Quick tips for your health

  • Eats your fruits and vegetables🥬
  • Sleep a good 9-11 hr if you are under the age of 16-18 for brain development and rest🧠
  • Drink an abundant amount of water (1-2 litres a day💧)
  • Eat your 5 fruits and vegetables a day consisting of all parts of the food plate e.g.-carbohydrates, proteins, fats,dairy,sugars,fruits etc.
